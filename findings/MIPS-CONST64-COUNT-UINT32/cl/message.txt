cmd/compile: test the full 64-bit constant shift count in MIPS lowering

The MIPS rules lowering shifts by a constant 64-bit count test uint32(c)
instead of uint64(c) (ARM and 386 use uint64(c)). A shift merge in late
opt after dec64 can produce a count >= 2^32, so (x>>7)>>(uint64(y)|
0xFFFFFFFF) is lowered as x>>6. Use uint64(c) in the constant-count shift
rules.

Fixes #82776.
