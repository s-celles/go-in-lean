cmd/compile: restore the complement in ARM MVNshiftRA constant folding

The ARM rule (MVNshiftRA (MOVWconst [c]) [d]) folds to int32(c)>>d,
dropping the bitwise NOT that the instruction performs (the LL and RL
siblings fold to ^(...), and go1.15 had ^int64(int32(c)>>d); the
complement was lost when the rules moved to typed AuxInts). The 32-bit
decomposition of Ctz64 reaches it through Zeromask lowered to SRAconst
[31] merged into MVN, so TrailingZeros64(0) evaluated to 32 instead of 64
when the low word folded to a constant during lowering.

Fixes #82786.
