cmd/compile: do not narrow an unsigned compare of a sign-extended value

The S390X rules that narrow 64-bit compares to 32 bits include
(CMPUconst x:(MOV(H|B)reg _) [c]) => (CMPWUconst x [c]). For a negative
sign-extended value the 64-bit register is larger than any zero-extended
immediate, but its low 32 bits can be smaller, so uint64(int8(-128)) <
0xFFFFFFFF folds to true. Restrict that instance to c>=0; the three other
instances (signed compare, or zero-extended value) stay.

Fixes #82774.
