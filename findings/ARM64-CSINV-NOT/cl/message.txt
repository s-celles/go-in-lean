cmd/compile: fold constant-flag CSINV to MVN, not the boolean Not

When the flags of a CSINV are a known constant and the condition is
false, the rule produces the generic op Not, which is the boolean
negation and lowers to XOR $1: a selected ^y becomes y^1. Produce MVN y
instead, matching the CSNEG (NEG) and CSINC (ADDconst [1]) siblings.

Fixes #82777.
